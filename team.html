The Anchor Education Initiative

We prioritize working with nonprofits that expand access to education, especially for underprivileged students navigating the college search with less information and fewer resources than their peers.

Adam and Jackson are both students of color who attended selective high schools and colleges, and know firsthand how much of getting there comes down to access: to information, to guidance, to knowing the right question to ask at the right time. That's not an abstract cause for us. It's why organizations like FairTest and A Better Chance are the kind of partners we go looking for, not just work we happen to take on.
